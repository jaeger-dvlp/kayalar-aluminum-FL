# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy has been prepared to protect the privacy of users who visit the website **www.kayalaraluminyum.com.tr**, operated by Kayalar Alüminyum Levha San. ve Tic. Ltd. Şti.

---

## Information Collected

The following information may be collected through our website:

- Name
- Surname
- Phone number
- E-mail address
- Company name
- Tax number
- Message content

This information is collected only through forms voluntarily filled out by users.

---

## Purposes of Using the Information

The collected information may be used for the following purposes:

- Responding to user inquiries
- Preparing quotations
- Establishing communication with users
- Improving service quality

---

## Data Security

Kayalar Alüminyum takes appropriate technical and administrative measures to ensure the security of user data.

---

## Third-Party Services

Limited analytical data may be collected on our website through **Google Tag Manager**.

This data is processed anonymously and is not associated with individual user identities.

---

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary.

The updated version becomes effective once it is published on our website.
