## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small data files stored in your browser by the websites you visit.

These files are used to improve the functionality and user experience of the website.

---

## Types of Cookies Used

### Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the proper functioning of the website.

### Analytical Cookies

Anonymous usage data may be collected through **Google Tag Manager** and similar tools.

This data is used for purposes such as:

- analyzing website performance
- improving user experience
